PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, clearly.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

OREVO 1.0 is designed as an offline-first application. The work-time information you enter is stored locally on your device. OREVO 1.0 does not require an OREVO account or cloud synchronization to use its core features.

1. What OREVO stores

To provide its core functionality, OREVO stores information you choose to enter or configure, such as work schedules, expected working time, overtime, recovered time, paid overtime, vacation, sick leave, days off, notes and related app preferences.

2. Where your data is stored

Core operational data in OREVO 1.0 is stored locally on your device. The application currently has no OREVO cloud account or cloud-sync service for this data.

3. Backups and exports

OREVO can create local backups and reports. When you choose to share or move an exported file using Android, the destination and handling of that file are controlled by you and by the app or service you select.

4. Accounts and authentication

OREVO 1.0 does not require you to create an OREVO account to use the application.

5. Permissions

OREVO aims to request only capabilities needed for features you deliberately use. Android or another selected application may present its own system prompts when you share, open, save or install files.

6. Third-party services

The core time-tracking experience is designed to work offline. If future versions introduce optional online services, analytics, accounts or synchronization, this policy will be updated before those changes are represented here as current behavior.

7. Data control and deletion

You control the records stored in the application. You can edit or delete entries within OREVO. Removing application data or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored data; create a backup first if you want to preserve it.

8. Security

Because operational data is stored on your device, device security matters. Use Android screen-lock and device-security features appropriate to your needs. OREVO 1.0 does not claim to provide a separate biometric application lock.

9. Children

OREVO is a work-time organization tool and is not designed specifically for children.

10. Changes

This policy may change as OREVO evolves. The revision date at the top identifies the current published version.

11. Contact

Creator: Bocoiu Filip. A dedicated public OREVO support contact will be added here before general public-store release.
